# Mavuka People Advisory: Presentation Business Bible

> **Demonstration data only.** This document combines public website information with intentionally fictional presentation assumptions. It is not a legal, financial, B-BBEE, tax, supplier, pricing or company-registration record. Replace every demo assumption with Mavuka-approved evidence before production.

## 1. Executive summary

Mavuka People Advisory is presented as a South African, executive-led Human Capital advisory partner for organisations navigating growth, restructuring, transformation and workforce change. It connects strategy to practical people, organisation, leadership and operating-model outcomes. The demonstration model focuses on six integrated service pillars: Fractional Human Capital Leadership; People and Culture Transformation; HR Function Optimisation; Organisational Design and Operating Model; Recruitment Services; and HR Practice Management Setup.

## 2. Company background, ownership and status

**Presentation assumption:** Mavuka People Advisory (Pty) Ltd is a privately owned South African professional-services company, founded in 2024, with a Johannesburg-based hybrid delivery model. The demonstration registration reference is 2024/487321/07. The demonstration depicts founder and director Gcobisa Ntshona as the majority owner and executive sponsor. ## 3. Products and service model

The model is advisory-led rather than product-led. Engagements start with a confidential discovery call, establish a business outcome and affected stakeholder groups, then move to a scoped proposal and delivery roadmap. Typical delivery modes in the demo are advisory retainers, leadership workshops, diagnostic sprints, project engagements and fractional leadership assignments. A client buys outcomes and specialist capacity, not a one-size-fits-all package.

## 4. Market and customer profile

The primary market is South African SMEs, scale-ups, mid-market businesses and corporate business units that need senior people capability without immediately building a large internal team. Target buyers are CEOs, founders, executive teams, CHROs, HR directors, transformation sponsors and operating leaders. The presentation model prioritises financial services, energy, professional services, technology, retail and growth-stage businesses; public material references experience across regulated and multinational environments.

## 5. Competition and positioning

Mavuka is positioned between a large consulting firm and a generalist HR provider: executive-level, boutique, context-led and practical. Its presentation differentiators are senior-led delivery, integrated people and operating-model thinking, commercial orientation, governance fluency and the ability to move from diagnosis to practical implementation support. Competitors may include large advisory firms, HR consultancies, executive-search specialists, independent HR consultants and in-house interim hires.

## 6. Marketing and sales model

The demonstration growth engine combines referral relationships, founder-led thought leadership, LinkedIn content, partner ecosystems, targeted executive conversations, speaking opportunities and website enquiries. The sales path is: enquiry; 30-minute chemistry/discovery conversation; optional diagnostic; tailored proposal; scope and commercial agreement; delivery launch; outcome review; referral or retainer conversation.

## 7. Operations and delivery

The presentation operating model is hybrid. A small core team owns client leadership, programme design and delivery, supported by a curated associate network for specialist requirements. Work is delivered remotely, at client sites or through leadership workshops. Each engagement has an executive sponsor, engagement lead, defined outcomes, milestones, stakeholder cadence and close-out review. Client data is handled through agreed confidentiality and information-sharing practices.

## 8. Management and people

The demo organisation is founder-led. The illustrative structure is: Founder and Managing Director; Principal Consultant; Associate Consultants; Talent and Research Partner; Project and Client Operations Coordinator; and specialist associates as required. It is deliberately lean, using senior attention where it matters and scalable specialist capacity where needed.

## 9. Strategic plan and milestones

The presentation plan is to establish a strong South African advisory base, deepen referral partnerships, create repeatable diagnostics and expand selective delivery into African markets. Illustrative milestones: 12 anchor clients, 8 retained-advisory relationships, a vetted associate panel and a flagship future-of-work leadership offering by the end of the first full operating year.

## 10. Financial plan and commercial model

**Presentation assumption:** the 2026 planning model targets R12 million annual revenue, a 34% gross margin and break-even during the second operating quarter. Revenue mix is illustrative: 40% transformation and organisation projects, 25% fractional leadership/retainers, 20% recruitment and talent advisory, and 15% workshops and diagnostics. Indicative demo pricing ranges: discovery diagnostic from R35,000; leadership workshop from R45,000; monthly advisory retainer from R55,000; and project work scoped by outcome, complexity and team size. These are fictional examples, not an offer or rate card.

## 11. Funding, risks and mitigation

The model assumes bootstrapped growth with working-capital discipline rather than a current funding request. Main risks are founder concentration, lumpy project revenue, long enterprise buying cycles, capacity pressure, data confidentiality, scope creep and reliance on associates. Demonstration mitigations are retainer mix, clear scopes, staged payment terms, documented delivery governance, associate agreements, secure collaboration practices and monthly pipeline reviews.
