# Mavuka Demonstration Service Playbooks

**Status: demo-only illustrative content.** These playbooks make the presentation feel complete; they are not approved operational claims and must be replaced or confirmed by Mavuka before production.

## 1. People Advisory

**When it fits:** a business is growing, changing direction, experiencing leadership pressure, lacks HR structure, needs a clearer people strategy or wants senior people guidance without deciding the answer in advance.

**Illustrative discovery questions:** What business outcome matters? What people friction is slowing it down? Which leaders and teams are affected? What current data, policies and capability already exist? What must stay confidential?

**Illustrative work:** people-strategy framing; workforce-planning discussion; leadership alignment; HR maturity assessment; governance and decision-rights review; HR operating-model options; practical roadmap and sponsor check-ins.

**Deliverables to confirm, not promise:** a leadership read-out, current-state view, prioritised roadmap, decision log, operating principles, implementation plan or retained advisory cadence.

**Boundary:** no legal opinion, payroll operation, technical HRIS configuration or employment-decision responsibility is implied.

## 2. Business Transformation

**When it fits:** a strategic shift, restructure, new operating model, merger-related change, culture challenge or transformation programme needs people-centred structure and momentum.

**Illustrative approach:** establish the business case; map sponsors, stakeholders and impacts; assess readiness; align leaders on behaviours and decisions; design communications and participation; sequence adoption support; review signals and adapt.

**Illustrative work:** organisation-design conversations; operating-model clarity; change-impact mapping; stakeholder engagement; leadership alignment; culture and behaviour discussion; change communications; readiness and adoption measures.

**Boundary:** results depend on client decisions and implementation. The demo does not promise a fixed methodology, speed, culture score or transformation outcome.

## 3. Recruitment and Talent

**When it fits:** a leadership, specialist or hard-to-fill role needs clearer definition, a more deliberate search approach or a stronger candidate experience.

**Illustrative approach:** clarify the role and success profile; agree decision-makers and process; discuss target talent market and attraction story; plan sourcing and assessment; coordinate feedback; support offer and onboarding hand-off.

**Illustrative work:** role brief; capability profile; search strategy; talent-attraction messaging; candidate journey; interview process; stakeholder calibration and hiring-process advisory.

**Boundary:** this demo does not claim active vacancies, a database, screening, guarantees, placement terms, hiring authority, geographic coverage or a particular fee model.

## 4. HR Function Setup

**When it fits:** a founder-led or scaling organisation needs a more consistent people foundation, or an established HR team needs clearer processes, governance and priorities.

**Illustrative approach:** understand business stage and risks; map the employee journey; identify priority processes; define roles, decision rights and service model; create an achievable improvement roadmap.

**Illustrative work:** HR-function design; people-process mapping; policy-framework priorities; HR governance; workforce-planning inputs; manager guidance; service model; capability uplift and implementation sequencing.

**Boundary:** Mavuka's demo does not provide legal drafting, statutory filing, payroll processing, disciplinary representation or HR-system configuration.

## 5. Digital and Workforce Readiness

**When it fits:** a new technology, AI initiative, platform rollout or digital transformation needs workforce readiness rather than only technical delivery.

**Illustrative approach:** identify impacted groups; understand capability and confidence; assess readiness; align leaders; shape messages; define learning and support needs; track adoption feedback.

**Illustrative work:** change-readiness conversation; stakeholder mapping; adoption and communication planning; learning-needs analysis; manager enablement; digital-skills discussion; workforce capability roadmap.

**Boundary:** technical architecture, software implementation, cybersecurity, systems integration and AI deployment remain outside this demo unless specifically approved.

## 6. Company, contact and commercial conversations

The safe response to any contact, supplier, availability, fee, location, B-BBEE, privacy, reference, case-study or commercial question is to explain the appropriate next step and invite confirmation. A consultation or quote request is the right action for tailored scope, schedule, fee, delivery location or supplier documentation.
