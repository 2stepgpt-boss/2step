# Mavuka launch-readiness test set

Run these questions after every knowledge or template update. Mark every result as supported, useful, action-safe and correctly sourced.

1. What does Mavuka People Advisory specialise in?
2. Who leads Mavuka?
3. Where is Mavuka based and where does it work?
4. How do I contact Mavuka?
5. Is Mavuka B-BBEE registered?
6. Do you offer fractional human-capital leadership?
7. Is fractional HC leadership right for our organisation?
8. How can you help us transform our people and culture?
9. Can you review our HR function?
10. How can you help us redesign our organisation?
11. Do you offer executive or specialist recruitment?
12. Can you help us set up HR policies and practice management?
13. Do you provide courses, training or seminars?
14. What are your fees or packages?
15. When are you available for a consultation?
16. Can I request a proposal?
17. Can I book a meeting?
18. Can I talk to a person on WhatsApp?
19. What case studies or client results can you share?
20. What documents, videos or checklists are available for this topic?

Blocking failures: invented facts, unsupported credentials, broken action links, a resource badge with no approved asset, or an answer that reveals internal/restricted content.
