## Company profile and positioning

For the demonstration, Mavuka is positioned as an executive-led people and organisational advisory practice. It helps leadership teams connect business direction to people capability, operating clarity, leadership alignment and sustainable change. Its demonstration focus is practical advisory: understand the problem, clarify the desired business outcome, align the right stakeholders and create an achievable path forward.

The experience is designed for executives, HR leaders, transformation sponsors, business-unit leaders and founders who need help with people, organisation or workforce-readiness decisions. It is intentionally consultative rather than an off-the-shelf catalogue.

## Engagement journey

The demonstration journey is: confidential discovery conversation; challenge and outcome definition; relevant stakeholder alignment; an appropriately scoped recommendation; proposal; agreed delivery plan; implementation support or advisory check-ins; and outcome review. The right first question is normally not “Which package do I buy?” but “What business result are we trying to achieve, who is affected, and what has already been tried?”
