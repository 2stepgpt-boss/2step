Grounding an answer means giving the assistant a defined source of truth. For article questions, that source should be the article and clearly identified supporting material—not an unbounded guess.

## Cite the source

Make it clear which article informed the answer. This supports trust, lets readers check the context, and keeps the conversation useful.
